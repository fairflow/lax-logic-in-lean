The fragment

RN(◯,{}) is the set of closed (atom-free) PLL formulas, quotiented by interderivability in LaxND. Write Γ ⊢ φ for PLL derivability and Γ ⊬ φ for its refutation.

Interd φ ψ := [φ] ⊢ ψ and [ψ] ⊢ φLaxLogic/Interd.lean:27

The order on classes is [φ] ≤ [ψ] ⟺ [φ] ⊢ ψ; strict order <, covering relation ⋖. The covering relation is written only in scoped form, a ⋖[K] b relative to a named finite set K:

a ⋖[K] b := a < b and ∀ c ∈ K. ¬(a < c < b)

An unscoped (genuine) cover cannot be settled by search: RN(◯,{}) is infinite, so ruling out every interposer is a statement about an infinite set — a proof obligation, not a test. The first genuine covers are now kernel-proved: ⊥ ⋖ ◯⊥ and ⊥ ⋖ ¬◯⊥ (bot_coversVF_obot, bot_coversVF_nbot). A scoped cover is destroyed by enlarging K; a genuine cover is permanent.

RN(◯,{}) is a bounded distributive lattice (meet/join the lifted ∧/∨; ⊥ and ⊤ = ⊥ ⊃ ⊥ are classes of the fragment itself), and genuine covers buy algebra: for any finite set U of genuine covers of n, the map S ⊆ U ⟼ n ∨ ⋁S order-embeds the Boolean lattice 2^U — the cube above n is free. The first full instance is proved: the bottom diamond {⊥, ◯⊥, ¬◯⊥, ◯⊥ ∨ ¬◯⊥} ≅ 2² (bottom_diamond, with the top corner identified as ρ4 — cube_bottomU_eq_rho4), via the bridge lemma covBy_of_coversVF transferring formula-level covers to class-level ⋖.

◯-depth

The syntactic ◯-depth of a formula is the maximal nesting of ◯:

boxDepth ⊥ = 0 boxDepth (A ∧ B) = boxDepth (A ∨ B) = boxDepth (A ⊃ B) = max (boxDepth A) (boxDepth B) boxDepth (◯A) = boxDepth A + 1wip/depth.lean

Classes take the minimum over their members — class depth, not syntactic depth, is the invariant:

DepthLe n A := ∃ B. Interd A B ∧ boxDepth B ≤ n

The depth-reducing laws (Families tab, last panel) collapse most constructions; the only depth-raising ones are ◯ over ∨ and ◯ over an implication whose consequent is not already boxed.

R, and membership in it

R is the ρ-catalogue: twenty-two closed formulas ρ0 … ρ21, held as data (formulas and a numbering, nothing asserted), with their pairwise distinctness certified separately. It is the reference set for everything on this page, and it is open-ended: RN(◯,{}) is infinite, so no finite R can be closed under the operations, and the tables locate where the current one runs out rather than pretending it does not.

φ ∈ R ⟺ ∃ k < 22. Interd φ (ρ k) φ ∉ R ⟺ ∀ k < 22. ¬ Interd φ (ρ k)

The second is the statement the tables mark ∉R: for every class of R, at least one direction of the would-be collapse is refuted. An ∉R cell is therefore a located new class, not a gap, and it is a statement about R, not about the formula: enlarging R can turn an ∉R verdict into a named class, and never the other way.

What sixteen ∉R cells do and do not say

  • Each is a located new class: the combination is interderivable with no ρk, and the verdict rests on 22 refutations, one per candidate.
  • They are not sixteen distinct classes. Whether two ∉R cells name the same class is a separate question, settled for only a few pairs; see Beyond R.
  • Nothing here says R is nearly closed. 201 cells are open, and an open cell may yet turn out to leave R too.

Regularities of the order

  • Graded. Every cover edge spans one rank; rank profile 1-2-2-3-5-5-3-1, height 7.
  • Ten poset-join-irreducibles: ρ0, ρ2, ρ3, ρ5, ρ7, ρ12, ρ13, ρ14, ρ19, ρ20. Every one that is not a rail atom is an implication.
  • Two stacked cubes. The interval [ρ4, ρ18] is exactly 2³ on atoms {ρ6, ρ7, ρ14}. The R tables confirm its identities: ρ6 ∧ ρ7 ≡ ρ7 ∧ ρ14 ≡ ρ6 ∧ ρ14 ≡ ρ4 and ρ9 ∨ ρ16 ≡ ρ9 ∨ ρ17 ≡ ρ16 ∨ ρ17 ≡ ρ18.
  • Two rails. The Rieger–Nishimura ladder at p := ◯⊥ is the left rail; the modal rail starts at b = ◯¬◯⊥ = ρ7 and meets it through the joins and the five ⊃-classes.
  • Local disjunction property. For x < u and y < u, u ⊢ x ∨ y ⟺ u ≡ x ∨ y. Every "is this join a new class?" is therefore one identity cell, which is exactly what the ∨ table now answers.

Poset lub is weaker than the class of the syntactic join. Whether v ≡ ⋁(lower covers of v) is a separate question per node, and the ∨ table settles it node by node.

Exactly what is certified

  • The 22 classes of R are pairwise distinct, kernel-backed.
  • The 16 ∉R cells are each certified outside R, on 22 refutations apiece.
  • The probe's 17 candidate classes are a count modulo the open cells recorded against each, and they are pairwise distinct only up to 22 open candidate-pairs. It is an upper bound: if an open pair collapses, the count falls.
  • How many distinct classes the 16 ∉R cells and the 17 candidates amount to together is OPEN. Only a few identifications are certified: the probe certified ρ7∨ρ13 ≡ ρ9∨ρ13 ≡ ρ12∧ρ15, ρ9∨ρ19 ≡ ρ5∨ρ19 ≡ ρ6∨ρ19, and ◯ρ6 ≡ ◯ρ9.

◯(g k) — one infinite antichain, not many small ones

Whether ◯(g k) is "many families of size-3 antichains": it is not. The record carries one theorem, uniform in the indices.

bg_incomparable : k ≥ 2, j ≠ k → ◯(g j) ⊬ ◯(g k) bg_pairwise : j, k ≥ 2, j ≠ k → ¬ Interd (◯(g j)) (◯(g k))wip/families.lean:535, 558

So { ◯(g k) : k ≥ 2 } is a single ℕ-indexed antichain: infinitely many classes, pairwise incomparable, each off the image and not ⊤ (bg_off_image, bg_not_top, both for k ≥ 2). It is drawn as one dotted row in Large-scale structure; a "size 3" reading counts the members a diagram happens to draw, not the members the theorem quantifies over.

The depth-reducing laws, for reference

Class depth, not syntactic depth, is the invariant: DepthLe n A := ∃ B. Interd A B ∧ boxDepth B ≤ n.

The only depth-raising constructions are ◯ over a disjunction and ◯ over an implication whose consequent is not already boxed (depth_raising_only_or_imp). And ◯ over ∨ genuinely escapes: distr_4_3_not_derivable refutes ◯(t3 ∨ t4) ⊢ ◯t3 ∨ ◯t4.

The certified rewrite set — machinery

A certified interderivability, oriented compound → representative, is a rewrite rule. Collect enough of them and the collection runs as a normaliser on any closed goal: congruence closure over facts already paid for. That is what Rewrite/ is.

Correctness does not depend on how effective it is: simplifyWith_interd is unconditional given that each rule's ok field is a proof. That is why the axiom pins below are a standing build-time guard rather than a convention.

The set is pegged to R. Its current extent: the rules act on ρ0–ρ12 and the two ◯-depth-2 formulas ◯ρ6 and ◯ρ11 — where they happened to be found first is history, not a boundary. No rule rewrites to anything outside R, and no unproved rule is in the set.

Standing rule: whenever R grows, the new class's kernel-proved interderivabilities (its identity cells in the R operation tables, once promoted from engine verdicts to Interd theorems) are added to the set; the set never shrinks. The 795 engine-classed R-table cells are the promotion queue — each classed cell ρi ∘ ρj ≡ ρk is a candidate rule awaiting its kernel Interd proof.

The "floor of 15" is not supported: measured bounds instead

The nested figure has been explained by saying the floor is 15, the number of classes the ∧/∨ closure cannot leave. That reasoning does not survive: 15 was a representative count, not a bound, and the number that matters is the number of distinct classes among the corpus's own values. Three measurements replace it, all made on this branch.

25 is a certified upper bound. The normaliser collapsing two forms is a proof that they are interderivable (simplifyWith_interd), so the 3,996 trees carry at most 25 distinct classes.

14 is a certified lower bound. Running the ≤5-world mutually confluent battery over the 25 residual forms separates 268 of the 300 pairs; 32 are left unresolved. The maximum set of forms that are pairwise separated has size 14, computed exactly and not greedily.

The closure lands inside R, as far as the tables reach. The corpus's ten generators are, in ρ terms, ρ0, ρ1, ρ2, ρ3, ρ4, ρ5, ρ7, ρ8, ρ9, ρ11. Closing them under the ∧ and ∨ tables reaches exactly 17 classes — ρ0–ρ11 together with ρ14, ρ15, ρ16, ρ17, ρ18 — and no ∉R cell at all. Four join cells inside that closure are open: ρ8 ∨ ρ11, ρ8 ∨ ρ15, ρ10 ∨ ρ11, ρ10 ∨ ρ15. So the closure has 17 classes if those four are classed, and more if any of them leaves R.

What rewriting cannot do

Rewriting is congruence closure over the banked equations, so a cell outside that closure is by construction out of reach: recombination cannot settle it, and only a new proof search or a larger countermodel can. That is the standing reason the 201 open cells of the R tables are search work, not bookkeeping. The simpset's value is throughput on the cells that are in the closure, and there the gain is about fivefold.
