The aim in one line

For every PLL formula φ and variable p there are p-free ∃p.φ and ∀p.φ. The interpolant interpF computes chains E_f (descending from ⊤) and A_f (ascending from ⊥). Soundness at every fuel is proved; cofinality is proved on the parking definition interpP, unconditional, verified 2026-09-05 (§4.17); with both, UI at a cell ⟺ the chains stabilise; stabilisation at every cell is the open theorem; the focalization bridge carries LJF◯ to PLL.

The statements

EStabilises p done   := Σ f₀, ∀ f ≥ f₀,  E_{f₀} ⊢ E_f  ×  E_f ⊢ E_{f₀}
AStabilises p done G := Σ f₀, ∀ f ≥ f₀,  E_f, A_{f₀} ⊢ A_f  ×  E_f, A_f ⊢ A_{f₀}

IsUIPair p done G E A :=
  E, A p-free;   Γ ⊢ E;   Δ, Γ ⊢ ψ → Δ, E ⊢ ψ   (p-free Δ, ψ)
                 A, Γ ⊢ G;  Δ, Γ ⊢ G → Δ, E ⊢ A   (p-free Δ)

N3:  EStabilises × AStabilises  ⟺  HasUI            (given N0a, N0d)
N4:  ∀ done G, Saturated done → ParkedCtx done → EStabilises × AStabilises
N6:  PLL_UI := ∀ p φ, Σ E A, IsUIPairPLL p φ E A

E_f := interpF p f [] done none      A_f := interpF p f [] done (some G)
