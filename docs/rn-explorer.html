Beyond it stretches an infinite strictly ascending chain — the boxed odd rungs ◯rn₃ < ◯rn₅ < ◯rn₇ < …, strict at every step (chain_step_strict), pairwise distinct, and from the third term on interderivable with no rung and not with ⊤ (complement_infinite): the complement of the ladder image is infinite.
