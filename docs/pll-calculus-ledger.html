How to read a pin

A claim is PROVED here only if it is sorry-free with a pinned #print axioms. Three pins occur:

propext — propositional extensionality only. The cleanest.

propext, Quot.sound — adds quotient soundness. Still choice-free, so the result survives extraction.

propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound — uses classical choice. Fine for a metatheorem, fatal for anything meant to run.

Terminology

An object of FRJ / FRJV is a disproof — regular or irregular. “Proof” is reserved for the provability calculi: Gbu◯, LaxND, G4c, SC. ProvableV G reads the wrong way round: it means G has an FRJV disproof.

§0The systems

Ten distinct systems, several of them repairs of one another. Confusing them has caused real errors, so the provenance is part of the record.

LaxNDF&M 1997 axioms
Natural deduction. Deriv Γ φ is what “PLL proves” means throughout the repository. The reference notion.
SCF&M Fig. 2 (Iemhoff’s GPLL)
Cut-free sequent calculus. Carries cut elimination, the subformula property and the disjunction property.
G4iLLIemhoff 2022/2024
Contraction-free refinement of G3iLL. Machine-checked incomplete for PLL here; contraction is not admissible.
G4cours — the repair
The calculus everything computational runs on: decider, countermodel emitter, interpolant tables.
DerivUF&M — PCLL
PLL plus the distribution scheme. Complete for mutually confluent constraint models.
DerivUNoFallPCLL (F&M) + ¬◯⊥ (ours)
PCLL plus the single closed axiom ¬◯⊥. It does not collapse ◯ — ◯ survives on atoms and implications, which is what ◯-normal form is for. What collapses is the variable-free fragment: infinite in PLL and PCLL (the ◯⊥ ladder), {⊥, ⊤} here.
LJF◯ours, after Liang & Miller
Focused calculus carrying the modal interpolant. Zero imports; its own rules throughout.
FRJ / FRJVFerrari & Fiorentini + our RefAt repair
Forward refutation calculus — its objects are disproofs. FRJV is the repaired V-family; it builds its countermodel from the disproof.
Gbu / Gbu◯Fiorentini & Ferrari §5 + our ◯-extension
The dual provability calculus. ◯-free Gbu is backtracking-free by querying a saturated FRJ database; Gbu◯ is complete for PLL via the LJF◯ translation (gbuC_complete, §4) — the database route to its completeness is retired.
Rejectours
Built-tree countermodel certificates; the refutation half of the two-sided engine.

§7Is Gbu◯ complete?

Three separate questions. Since the evening of 31 August, all three have answers.

For IPC formulas — yes.

search (Theorem 8) plus gbu_frj_duality (Theorem 9) give ProvableGbu G ↔ ¬ ProvableV G on the ◯-free fragment, and provableV_of_not_pll closes the loop to IPL. provableGbuC_iff_provableGbu then transfers all of it to Gbu◯, since the two calculi coincide on ◯-free G.

As an equivalence with the other presentations — partly.

The conservativity provableGbuC_iff_provableGbu is proved and pinned at [propext], and it is enforced as a compiler gate: the erasure functions deCircR / deCircI must stay total, so an ill-formed ◯ rule breaks the build. Negative-tested three times.

For PLL — yes: gbuC_complete, proved 31 August (evening).

Not by the search route. residues_unsatisfiable had shown searchO’s two supplies to be contradictory — the theorem asserted nothing for any modal G and was retired — and the duality-gap record (§6) stands as the reason the route changed. Completeness goes instead through LJF◯ focalisation: bridge_iff hands every PLL theorem a focused derivation, and tInv (wip/gbu_ljfo.lean) translates it into Gbu◯.

The translation’s shape was forced by two kernel-checked screens — GbuIC is not monotone (gbuIC_not_monotone) and has no modus ponens (gbuIC_no_mp) — so it is a CPS hoisting, mode-generic over a step kit: regular left rules above any ◯-opening, the irregular ◯-goal rules below it, with lax spines built entirely inside the irregular judgment and lcircI as the context bridge the regular judgment lacks. The licenced |◯C| adaptation of L⊃ᵢ is consumed at exactly one site, irrKit.impOpen.

FRJW W1–W4 remain banked as the disproof side; whether to rebuild an FRJW search on top of the completed calculus is a separate decision.

§8What the ρ-sweep settled

All 462 directed ρ-cells were run blind through the V-engine and joined against the RNDB bank (tools/frjv-sweep-check.py). 280 cells carry a kernel-checked ¬ Deriv [ρi] ρj; the engine hit 274 and missed six.

The six are budget artefacts, not incompleteness.

The sweep prints lamCapped and dbCapped, and both were false — which I read as convergence. But Config has two more caps it does not print: jmax := 3, the join arity cap, and pmax := 2, the promise-component cap. Re-running ρ12 ⊃ ρ18 with the flags exposed gives jmaxBinding = true, pmaxBinding = true. The engine only ever formed premise families of total size ≤ 3, out of 60 irregular rows available.

So no incompleteness has been found in the closed fragment, and the standing rule holds exactly as written: a miss is not-found-within-bound, never a verdict.
