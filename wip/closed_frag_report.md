# The closed (variable-free) fragment of PCLL, stratified by crank

**Question.** PCLL = PLL + the distribution scheme ◯(A∨B) ⊃ (◯A∨◯B);
derivability from premises is `PLLND.ConfluentU.DerivU`
(LaxLogic/PLLConfluentComplete.lean).  Complexity is `crank`
(`PLLND.SemUI.crank`, LaxLogic/PLLSemUILayered.lean): atoms and ⊥ cost
0, ∧/∨ take the maximum, ⊃ adds 1, ◯ adds 2.  Is there an R₀ ≤ 7 such
that every `DerivU`-interderivability class of closed formulas with a
representative of crank ≤ 7 already has a representative of
crank ≤ R₀ — equivalently, does the class count stop growing as crank
increases?

**Answer: no collapse at or below crank 7.**  New classes appear at
*every* crank stratum 0–7.  The counts through crank 5 are exact
(every classification cell settled); crank 6 and 7 are certified lower
bounds (unsettled cells are FLAGGED, below).

| crank R | new classes at R | classes with min-crank ≤ R | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | exact (flag-free) |
| 1 | 1 | 2 | exact (flag-free) |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | exact (flag-free) |
| 3 | 2 | 5 | exact (flag-free) |
| 4 | 2 | 7 | exact (flag-free) |
| 5 | 4 | 11 | exact (flag-free) |
| 6 | 8 | 19 | lower bound (26 flags) |
| 7 | 3 | 22 | lower bound (140 flags) |

Precisely:

* **R₀ ≤ 5 fails unconditionally.**  Strata 0–6 give classes of
  minimal crank exactly 6 (e.g. the class of
  ◯¬◯⊥ ⊃ (¬◯⊥ ∨ ◯⊥), the G4iLL-blocker shape `q8`): the crank-≤5
  strata were closed to a fixpoint with *no* unsettled cell, so every
  closed formula of crank ≤ 5 is interderivable with one of the 11
  crank-≤5 representatives, and `q8` is separated from each of the 11
  by a mutually confluent countermodel.
* **R₀ = 6 fails modulo the 26 stratum-6 flags.**  Three classes of
  minimal crank 7 were found (`r19`, `r20`, `r21` below), each
  separated from *all* 19 discovered crank-≤6 classes; all 57
  separating cells are **kernel-pinned** in `wip/closed_frag_pins.lean`
  (`#print axioms`: propext, Quot.sound only).  The caveat: 26
  crank-6 cells stayed unsettled, so a never-generated crank-6 member
  of one of these classes cannot be excluded (see *Flags*).

## Method

The Lindenbaum algebra of the closed fragment is generated by ⊥ under
∧, ∨, ⊃, ◯, and `DerivU`-interderivability is a congruence for all
four connectives, each monotone in the cranks of its arguments.  So a
closed formula of crank ≤ R is interderivable with a crank-≤R
combination of minimal-crank class representatives, and the stratified
closure (at each R = 0…7, close the representative set under the
connectives keeping results of crank ≤ R, to a fixpoint) reaches a
representative of every class with a crank-≤7 member — *provided every
cell it meets is settled*; each FLAG punctures the argument above its
stratum (a flagged candidate is never used as a generator).

Verdicts (three-valued; run compiled, one appended line per cell):

* **separated** — the candidate's forcing vector differs from a
  representative's on the confluent battery (all 9075 well-formed
  mutually confluent frames on ≤ 4 worlds + the 1459 canonical rooted
  5-world orbits, exhaustive at 5 by the generated-submodel argument).
  A disagreement world is a mutually confluent finite countermodel to
  one entailment direction — exactly what
  `PLLND.RNC.not_derivU_of_checkConf` consumes, kernel-escalatable
  cell by cell.
* **≡** — both directions found by the positive tier ladder of
  `wip/rnc_probe.lean` (plain PLL at 20000 nodes; one, then two
  cut-style distribution applications at 15000/12000), sound for
  `DerivU` by `PLLND.RNC.derivU_of_proved`.
* **FLAG** — vector-identical on the whole battery, interderivability
  not certified within budget.  Escalated once (100000/60000/40000
  ladder of `phaseR`); survivors reported, never dropped.

Cheap syntactic dedup: constant folding (⊥∧φ=⊥, ⊤∧φ=φ, ⊥∨φ=φ, ⊤∨φ=⊤,
⊥⊃φ=⊤, ⊤⊃φ=φ, φ⊃⊤=⊤, φ⊃φ=⊤), idempotence, canonical ∧/∨ argument
order, ◯◯φ=◯φ, ◯⊤=⊤ — each a plain-PLL interderivability, none
raising crank.  Corpus replay: the 19 dictionary candidates
q0…q14, w15…w18 (wip/rnc_probe.lean) were injected as seeds at their
crank strata.

**Skips and caps (all of them).**  The seed q13 = ◯(◯¬◯⊥ ⊃ (¬◯⊥∨◯⊥))
was skipped (crank 8 > cap 7; reported in the stream).  No round cap
was hit: every stratum reached its in-stratum fixpoint in ≤ 3 rounds.
7 cells exceeded the 3 s slow-cell threshold (logged inline).  Sweep:
680 cells (492 ≡, 22 NEW, 166 FLAG) in ~12 min; escalation ~11 min.

## Pivot answers (item 4 of the brief)

* **◯⊤**: `DerivU`-equivalent to ⊤ (crank 3 collapses to crank 1).
  Plain PLL both ways: ◯⊤ ⊃ ⊤ is trivial and ⊤ ⊢ ◯⊤ is `laxIntro`;
  found at tier 0.
* **¬¬◯⊥** (crank 4): equivalent to **no** smaller-crank closed
  formula.  Separated from each of the five crank-≤3 classes
  (⊥, ⊤, ◯⊥, ¬◯⊥, ¬◯⊥∨◯⊥) by mutually confluent countermodels; since
  strata ≤ 3 are flag-free-complete, this is unconditional.
  Kernel pins already in the tree, `wip/rncCert.lean`:
  `rnc_ref_6_0`, `rnc_ref_1_6`, `rnc_ref_6_2`, `rnc_ref_6_3`,
  `rnc_ref_6_4`.
* **◯¬◯⊥** (crank 5): equivalent to **no** smaller-crank closed
  formula.  Separated from each of the seven crank-≤4 classes; strata
  ≤ 4 flag-free-complete, so unconditional.  Kernel pins:
  `rnc_ref_5_0`, `rnc_ref_1_5`, `rnc_ref_5_2`, `rnc_ref_5_3`,
  `rnc_ref_5_4`, `rnc_ref_5_6`, `rnc_ref_5_7` (wip/rncCert.lean).
  The separating cells (with pinned models) are also printed in the
  pivots section of wip/closed_frag_out.txt.

## What distribution actually does at crank ≤ 7

Only four equivalences in the whole run needed the scheme (everything
else was plain PLL):

* ◯(¬¬◯⊥ ∨ ¬◯⊥) ≡ q9, i.e. **q12 ≡ q9** (one application; this was
  the one merge already known from the 19-candidate probe);
* ◯(¬¬◯⊥ ∨ ◯¬◯⊥) = ◯q9 ≡ q9 and ◯((¬¬◯⊥⊃◯⊥) ∨ ¬¬◯⊥) = ◯q11 ≡ q11
  (one application each): ◯ is *idempotent on the ∨-classes* under
  distribution;
* **w17 ≡ w16** (single instance premise, escalation): with
  w15 ≡ w16 and w18 ≡ w16 (both plain PLL through the normalized
  representative), the four closure witnesses of the old probe merge
  into the single class r14 — resolving cells that
  wip/rnc_out.txt / rnc_r_out3.txt had left UNKNOWN.

## Flags (all of them; never dropped)

166 raised (0 at strata 0–5, 26 at stratum 6, 140 at stratum 7); 57
resolved by the escalation ladder (56 forward directions by plain PLL
at budget 100000, 1 — the w17 cell — by a single distribution-instance
premise at 60000; backward directions: 41 already proved at sweep
tiers, 16 by plain PLL at 100000).  **109 final flags**, each of the
form "candidate ?≡ rep": vector-identical to exactly one
representative on the entire ≤5-world confluent battery, but not
proved interderivable within the budgets.  Distribution over
representatives (count ?≡ rep): r1:21, r10:21, r21:20, r15:13, r20:7,
r8:6, r11:5, r12:4, r13:3, r17:3, r9:3, r14:2, r16:1.

Semantics of a surviving flag: if genuinely inequivalent, the pair is
indistinguishable below 6 worlds, so settling it needs either a
≥6-world confluent countermodel or a deeper positive search.  Flags
cannot *reduce* the class counts (every NEW is separation-certified);
they mark possible additional classes at cranks 6–7 and are the only
gap in the completeness argument at those strata.  The cluster at r1
(⊤) and at the ∨-tops r10, r15, r21 is the closed-fragment analogue of
the top-of-lattice region where the old probe's unknowns also lived.
