# TOOLS.md — the tool register

The register of the **latest recommended versions** of the working tools:
tactics/normalisation, proof search, countermodel search, database
extension and maintenance. `CLAUDE.md` points here and carries no tool
detail of its own; this file is allowed to be very specific **because it
is kept current**.

**Update rule.** Any commit that changes a registered tool's behaviour
updates that tool's version cell in the same commit. Version cells are
`branch @ hash · date` of the last commit touching the tool's path —
**regenerated, never hand-edited**: run

    tools/tools-register.sh

and paste its output over the cells. A row whose printed hash differs
from the cell here is stale; fix it before relying on the row.

2026-10-22 @ `bc86397`. Rows whose tool path the script does not list
(the FRJV sweep, `#axiom_sweep`, the two estates, §7, §8) keep their

## 2 · Proof search

**Routing: INTERIM** (2026-10-22), until the pre-registered comparison
(gate G4) orders the lean-certify decider settings. Measured facts only.

* To decide a PLL formula, closed or with variables (a sequent through its
  implication form), use **`pllfast`** first (§3). It runs the subframe
  tableau, with proof terms through `termOf` and countermodels minimised, and
  falls back to the FRJW engine. It is the fastest route on the 33-cell
  benchmark of 2026-10-15/22 (the 26 benchmark cells and the 7 refbench
  cells; §3, rows `pllfast` and the subframe tableau).
* **`subtabdecide`** (§3) is the lean-certify decider form of the same
  tableau.
* **`pll`** (§3) is the FRJW route (W-engine, then `checkClosed`), with its
  own output layer.
* **G4c** (`pll_g4c`, §8, and the G4c engines below) is for proof terms in
  premise-loaded (PCLL/`DerivU`) work. Its failures are never refutations.
* The **two-sided engine** (below) is a specialist: `tools/Engines.lean`
  (`ljfoProve`) records that on larger goals the ordering against G4c
  reverses, and its refutation side, `Reject.certifies`, filters a battery,
  which the engine register marks `mayDiscover := false`
  (`Engines.rejectCheck`).
* **FRJV is excluded.**
